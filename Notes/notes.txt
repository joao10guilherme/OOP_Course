-> Recursive Functions

 . Calls itself.
 . A recursive functions devides the problem into two conceptual pieces: the base case (when the functions
    doesn't call itself again), and the recursive case (when the function solves a small part of the problem and
    calls a fresh copy of itself to work on the smaller problem).
